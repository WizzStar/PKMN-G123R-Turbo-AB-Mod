# G123R Turbo_AB Mod (v1.0.0)

Hold **A** or **B** to auto-advance dialogue and battle text — no more mashing through long conversations. Works natively across **Gen 1, Gen 2, and Gen 3** (Red, Blue, Yellow, Gold, Silver, Crystal, FireRed, LeafGreen, Ruby, Sapphire, Emerald).

## What it does

- **Hold A or B to fast-forward text** — advances NPC dialogue, signs, items, and battle messages automatically.
- **Smart Safety** — pauses automatically on YES/NO prompts, choice menus, and battle commands so you don't misclick.
- **Overworld & Soft-Reset Safe** — won't re-talk to NPCs in a loop, and pauses while holding Start/Select so soft-resetting still works.
- **Customizable** — tweak initial delay and speed (1–5) in mod settings.

## Settings

- **TURBO A / TURBO B**: Toggle rapid-fire for each button individually.
- **HOLD DELAY**: 16 frames (~267 ms) before auto-advance starts.
- **REPEAT SPEED**: 1–5 ladder (Default is **3** / 10 frames between repeats).

## How to Install

1. Download `wizzstar_turbo_ab_v1_0_0.zip`.
2. Enable it under **MODS** in your game launcher.
